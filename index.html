Computational Thinking and Creative Computing through Drawing

In this class, I explore how drawing practice can develop computational thinking.

We study Dada’s chance operations, instruction-based Conceptual Art, Systems Art, participatory practices and early Computer Art to see how artists use rules to organise a process and leave room for interpretation and unexpected results.

Drawing is both a medium for inquiry and an object of study. Students use drawing to formulate, test and revise rules. They trace how a drawing takes shape as people interpret a score, code generates paths and a plotter executes the instructions through different materials.

Recreate the Past

Component 03 · Weeks 12–15

Robotic Drawing and Material Exploration

In Little Drawing Machine, students test materials across three p5.js sketches from Recreate the Past. They select one sketch and use a shared SVG at the same scale to make at least three mounted works with different material combinations.
